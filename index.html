Research & Planning
Months 1–2· In progress· Est. cost: $15,000–30,000 MXN
Why this matters: Everything downstream depends on getting this right. The decisions you make here — business model, location zone, test menu focus — set the ceiling for revenue and speed-to-permit. Rushing past this phase is the #1 mistake new lab operators make in Mexico.
Core tasks
◎
Market research — Querétaro metro
Map out every competitor by zone: Juriquilla, Centro, Satélite, El Pueblito, Corregidora. Count locations, note pricing, identify wait times and test menu gaps. Visit at least 5 labs as a "mystery patient."
1–2 weeksGo in person
◎
Define business model
Decide on revenue mix: direct walk-in patients, physician referral network, corporate health contracts (empresas), IMSS subrogación (advanced). Most successful Querétaro labs start with physician referrals + walk-in, then add corporate.
Start with 2 channels max
◎
Financial modeling
Build a 3-year P&L projection. Key inputs: avg ticket ~$500–700 MXN, 150–250 studies/mo year 1, reagent cost ~35% of revenue. Model conservative, base, and optimistic scenarios.
$5,000 if outsourcedUse the Financials tab
◎
Location shortlist (3 sites minimum)
Requirements: min 60m², ground floor preferred, parking or near public transit, zoned for servicios médicos. Check uso de suelo with Municipio before signing anything. Ideally near medical offices or clinics.
2–3 weeksVerify uso de suelo first
◎
Define initial test menu
Start with the 50–80 highest-demand studies: BHC, QS (6, 12, 27), perfil lipídico, perfil tiroideo, uroanálisis general, prueba de embarazo, cultivos básicos. Specialty panels (hormonas, tumor markers) can be added later or referred out.
Match menu to equipment
◎
Get 3 competitor price lists
Salud Digna publishes online. For Chopo and local labs, visit or call. You need these to set your own pricing tier — aim for 20–40% above Salud Digna, 30–50% below hospital labs.

Key decisions to make
General vs. specialty?
General labs have broader demand but thinner margins. Specialty (e.g., hormonal panels, molecular) commands higher tickets but needs more expensive equipment and a wider referral base. Recommended: start general, layer specialty at month 12–18.
Own vs. lease equipment?
Many reagent companies (Roche, Beckman, Mindray) offer comodato: free analyzer in exchange for reagent exclusivity. This slashes startup cost but locks you in. Good for year 1, renegotiate after.
Referral lab partnership?
For tests you won't run in-house (PCR, specialized cultures, genetic), partner with a referral lab like Carpermor or Quest Mexico. You draw the sample, they run it, you keep the margin on the patient-facing price.
Querétaro-specific tips
Best zones to research: Juriquilla/Zibatá (high income, low competition), Satélite/Epigmenio (dense residential, IMSS overflow), and Centro/Constituyentes (high foot traffic, physician offices).

Corporate channel: Querétaro's industrial parks (El Marqués, Aeropuerto) house 300+ manufacturing plants required by NOM to provide annual employee health screenings. This is a massive untapped B2B channel.

Legal & Corporate Setup
Months 2–4· To do· Est. cost: $30,000–60,000 MXN
Critical path: You cannot apply for COFEPRIS permits without a legally constituted empresa and RFC. Don't delay this — every week of delay here pushes your entire launch timeline.
Core tasks
⚖
Choose business structure
S.A. de C.V. is recommended for a clinical lab. It provides liability protection, a clear corporate structure for investor/partner entry, and is the expected format for COFEPRIS applications. Avoid Persona Física — too much personal liability.
S.A. de C.V. recommended
⚖
Hire notario público
The notario drafts and formalizes the Acta Constitutiva (founding charter). You'll need: company name (razón social), partner details, capital structure, object social (must include "servicios de laboratorio de análisis clínicos").
$15,000–25,000 MXN2–4 weeks
⚖
Register RFC with SAT
Tax ID registration at sat.gob.mx. Choose régimen: likely Régimen General de Ley for an S.A. de C.V. You'll need the e.firma (formerly FIEL) and the Acta Constitutiva. This enables CFDI (electronic invoicing).
1–2 weekssat.gob.mx
⚖
Open business bank account
Bring: Acta Constitutiva, RFC, official ID of legal representative, comprobante de domicilio. BBVA, Santander, and Banorte all offer PyME accounts with reasonable fees. Open before equipment purchases.
1 week
⚖
IMPI trademark registration (optional)
Register your lab name and logo with IMPI (Instituto Mexicano de la Propiedad Industrial). Not legally required, but protects your brand if you plan to grow or franchise. Filing online costs ~$2,500 MXN.
~$2,500 MXN3–6 months for approval
⚖
Sign lease contract
Critical: the lease must specify "uso de suelo: servicios médicos" or equivalent. COFEPRIS will check this. Negotiate 6-month rent-free buildout period if possible. Ensure landlord agrees to required modifications (plumbing, ventilation, waste room).
Uso de suelo required$18,000–35,000/mo in Juriquilla

Razón social — naming tips
Before the notario starts, check name availability at the Secretaría de Economía portal. Avoid generic names that IMPI might reject. Include "Laboratorio" or "Lab" for instant recognition. Think about how it looks on Google Maps and lab results letterhead.
Object social wording: Make it broad enough to cover future services. Example: "Prestación de servicios de laboratorio de análisis clínicos, toma de muestras, imagenología, y servicios de salud en general." This avoids needing amendments later.

COFEPRIS & NOM Permits
Months 3–9· Critical path — allow 6+ months· Est. cost: $20,000–80,000 MXN
This is the longest and most unpredictable phase. COFEPRIS timelines are notoriously variable. Officially 60–120 business days for the Licencia Sanitaria, but real-world timelines in 2024–2025 range from 4 to 12 months. Start this the moment you have your RFC and lease. Do not wait for buildout to be complete — the application process runs in parallel.
Core tasks
!
Hire / contract Director Técnico (QFB)
This is the single most critical hire in the entire project. COFEPRIS will not issue a Licencia Sanitaria without a named QFB (Químico Farmacéutico Biólogo) with an active cédula profesional. They can be full-time or part-time (some labs share). Salary range: $18,000–35,000 MXN/mo full-time.
Must do first$18K–35K/mo
!
Licencia Sanitaria — COFEPRIS
The main operating permit. Application via SIVISAM portal requires: RFC, lease, floor plan, equipment list with serial numbers, QFB cédula profesional, NOM-087 RPBI plan, and proof of waste carrier contract. COFEPRIS will schedule a physical inspection of the premises.
Critical path3–9 months$5,000–15,000 MXN
⚖
Aviso de Funcionamiento
Filed online via the COFEPRIS SIVISAM portal. This is a notification that you intend to operate — simpler than the Licencia but must be done before opening. Requires CURP, RFC, and facility address.
1–2 weekscofepris.gob.mx
⚖
NOM-007-SSA3-2011 compliance
Infrastructure standard for clinical labs. Defines minimum areas: reception, sample collection (toma de muestra), analysis, wash/sterilization, results delivery. Min 60m². Separate RPBI storage area required.
Plan floor layout early
⚖
NOM-166-SSA1-1997 compliance
Quality standard: SOPs for every test, equipment calibration logs, internal quality control records, external quality assessment (EQA) participation, and staff competency documentation.
QFB usually owns this
⚖
NOM-087 RPBI waste plan
Biohazardous waste (Residuos Peligrosos Biológico-Infecciosos): separation by color-coded containers, labeled sharps disposal, contracted authorized waste carrier (e.g., BioSeguridad). Carrier must have SEMARNAT permit.
Carrier: $3,000–8,000/mo
⚖
COFEPRIS physical inspection
After filing, COFEPRIS schedules an on-site inspection. They check: floor plan vs. application, equipment in place and calibrated, QFB present and documented, RPBI plan posted, SOPs available. If deficiencies are found, they give a window to correct — but this adds months.

Pro tips from the field
Start the QFB search immediately. Good QFBs in Querétaro are in demand. Post on QFB Facebook groups, contact UAQ's Facultad de Química. Some labs hire a QFB early as a consultant ($8K–12K/mo part-time) to help prepare the COFEPRIS application — this often pays for itself by avoiding costly inspection deficiencies.

Infrastructure & Equipment
Months 6–10· To do· Est. cost: $900,000–1,200,000 MXN
Biggest capital outlay. Equipment and buildout represent 55–65% of total startup cost. The comodato (reagent-rental) model can reduce upfront equipment cost by 40–60%, but locks you into a single reagent supplier. Evaluate carefully.
Core tasks
▦
Lab buildout & remodeling
Per NOM-007: separate areas for reception/waiting, toma de muestras (phlebotomy), analytical area, wash/sterilization, RPBI storage, and restrooms. Surfaces must be non-porous, easy to disinfect. Adequate ventilation and lighting per COFEPRIS standards.
$200,000–400,000 MXN4–8 weeks
▦
Equipment procurement — analyzers
Core equipment: hematology analyzer (Sysmex, Mindray), chemistry analyzer (Beckman AU480, Roche c311, Mindray BS-200), coagulation, urinalysis, centrifuges (x2 min), refrigerators (2–8°C for reagents, -20°C for samples), and microscope for manual differentials.
$500,000–800,000 MXN (purchase)Ask about comodato
▦
Cold chain & sample storage
Reagent fridge (2–8°C), sample freezer (-20°C), temperature monitoring logs (daily, documented). Some COFEPRIS inspectors now expect digital temperature logging. UPS/no-break for fridges is strongly recommended.
$40,000–80,000 MXNTemperature log required
▦
LIS (Laboratory Information System)
Software to manage orders, results, patient records, billing. Options: LabSoft, HealthPath, Labcore, or custom-built. Must support CFDI invoicing and PDF result delivery. Integration with analyzer middleware is ideal but not mandatory at launch.
$50,000–120,000 MXNStart simple, upgrade later
▦
IT infrastructure
Internet (redundant: main + backup), network for LIS, printer for results, patient check-in system. Optional: patient self-service kiosk, WhatsApp Business integration for result delivery.
$20,000–40,000 MXN
▦
Furniture, fixtures & signage
Reception desk, waiting area seating, phlebotomy chairs (reclinable, padded), lab benches (stainless or epoxy-top), external signage, and branded interior. First impression matters — patients choose with their eyes.
$60,000–120,000 MXN

Equipment decision: buy vs. comodato
Purchase outright: Full control over reagent sourcing, negotiate better prices over time, no volume commitments. But: $500K–800K upfront for analyzers alone. Best if you have the capital or can finance through Nafin/bank.
Comodato (reagent-rental): Analyzer provided free by the reagent company (Roche, Beckman, Mindray) in exchange for exclusive reagent purchases with minimum monthly volumes. Slashes startup cost by $400K–600K. Drawback: you're locked in for 3–5 years and pay above-market reagent prices. Best for capital-constrained startups. Renegotiate aggressively at renewal.

Staffing & Training
Months 8–12· To do· Monthly payroll: $75,000–120,000 MXN
Your team is your brand. In the mid-market segment, patient experience is the differentiator. A warm recepcionista, a skilled phlebotomist with a gentle touch, and a QFB who stands behind every result — that's what generates word-of-mouth referrals in Querétaro's tight-knit medical community.
Core hires
★
Director Técnico — QFB
Legally required. Responsible for quality, SOPs, result validation, and COFEPRIS compliance. Must have active cédula profesional from DGP. This person's name goes on the Licencia Sanitaria — choose carefully.
Required by law$18,000–35,000/mo
+
Químico / Lab technician (1–2)
Run day-to-day analyses, maintain equipment, prepare reagents, log QC data. Ideally QFB or TLC (Técnico Laboratorista Clínico) with cédula. Can handle phlebotomy if trained.
$10,000–18,000/mo each
+
Phlebotomist (flebotomista)
Dedicated sample collection specialist. Critical for patient experience — a painful draw = lost patient. Training in NOM-087 handling and pediatric draws is a plus. Can also handle home collection (toma a domicilio).
$8,000–12,000/moHire for soft skills
+
Recepcionista / Admin
Patient intake, scheduling, billing, CFDI issuance, result delivery coordination. This is the face of your lab. Must be organized, warm, and capable of handling LIS data entry accurately.
$8,000–12,000/mo
○
Cleaning / maintenance (part-time or outsourced)
Lab cleaning must follow infection control protocols — not standard janitorial. RPBI areas need specific handling. Consider outsourcing to a specialized medical cleaning company initially.
$4,000–6,000/mo
Training requirements
▤
NOM-087 RPBI handling
All staff must be trained on biohazardous waste classification, container color codes, sharps disposal, and spill protocols. Document training with dates and signatures — COFEPRIS checks this.
▤
Quality control procedures
Daily QC runs on all analyzers, Levey-Jennings charts, corrective actions when out of range. The QFB should lead this training and establish SOPs before day 1 of operations.
▤
Patient interaction & privacy
Confidentiality of results (NOM-004-SSA3 clinical records), proper patient identification (2 identifiers), informed consent for HIV and genetic testing, fasting verification protocols.
▤
LIS / software training
All staff touching the system need training on order entry, result input, report generation, and basic troubleshooting. Schedule 2–3 days before opening with the LIS vendor.

Hiring tips for Querétaro
Where to find QFBs: UAQ (Universidad Autónoma de Querétaro) Facultad de Química graduates ~40 QFBs/year. Also check UNAM distance programs, Facebook groups ("QFB Querétaro Empleos"), and LinkedIn. Offer flexible hours — many QFBs split time between multiple labs.

Retain with culture: Small labs compete on culture, not salary. Offer training budgets, clear growth paths, and a say in SOPs. A QFB who feels ownership of quality stays longer.

Revenue starts here. The soft launch is your chance to iron out workflows with real patients before marketing at full volume. Start with physician referrals (your highest-trust channel), then layer in walk-ins and corporate. Your first 100 patients define your reputation.

Competitive edge playbook
Resultado en 4 horas: This is your killer differentiator. Salud Digna takes 24–48h. Hospital labs take 24h+. If you can promise and deliver same-morning results for common panels (BHC, QS, lipid), physicians will refer to you over everyone.

WhatsApp results + home collection = the modern Mexican patient's ideal experience. No one else in mid-market Querétaro is doing both well.

Physician loyalty: Send monthly "thank you" summaries to referring doctors showing volume referred. Make them feel like partners, not just referral sources.
